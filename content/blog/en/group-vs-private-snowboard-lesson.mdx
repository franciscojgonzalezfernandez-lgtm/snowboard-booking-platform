I don't only teach private. I teach private lessons — for you or your small group who book together — and larger group lessons for friends or companies. The question isn't which is "better", but what you're after that day.

## The private lesson: the focus is yours

It's just you, or your small group (1 to 4) who book together and already know each other. All the time is yours: I watch your turns, pick the one thing holding you back, and work on it with you, on video. It's the way to **learn fast and tailored to you**, or to unstick something specific. I group by level so the lesson actually moves.

## The group lesson: dynamic, social and cheaper

A bigger group — friends, a birthday, a company team. You learn differently: **you have fun, it's more dynamic, and you also learn from other people's mistakes** (watching someone fix a skid teaches you as much as doing it yourself). And it's **cheaper per head**. Ideal for having a good time while you learn together.

## Which suits you

- You want to improve fast, something specific, at your own pace → **private**.
- You're a group of friends or a company team and want fun while you learn, at the best per-person price → **group**.
- Both? Plenty of people do a group lesson to get hooked, then a private one to make the jump.
