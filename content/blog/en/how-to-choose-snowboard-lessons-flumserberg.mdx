Choosing a lesson shouldn't be a leap of faith. Whoever you end up booking with, the same handful of questions tell you what you're really getting. Here's the checklist I'd use as a parent or a beginner — written to judge *any* lesson in Flumserberg, with honest answers for how mine work so you can compare like for like.

## 1. How many riders per instructor?

This is the big one. The more riders share a coach, the more of your session is spent waiting. Ask for the hard maximum, not the average. *(Here: capped at four, never more.)*

## 2. Do they group by level, or just by who booked?

A lesson that mixes a first-timer with a confident rider moves at nobody's pace. Ask whether they group by ability. *(Here: always by level. Mixed-level groups get split or steered to a private slot before we start.)*

## 3. Is the instructor certified, and in what languages?

Ask about certification and which languages they teach in — learning something physical in your own language matters more than people expect. *(Here: certified coaching in English, German or Spanish.)*

## 4. What does the price include — and exclude?

Get this in writing. What's the price *for the slot*, does it cover the whole group or per head, and what's on top? *(Here: one flat price for up to four riders — CHF {{ONE_HOUR}} one hour, CHF {{TWO_HOURS}} two hours, CHF {{INTENSIVE}} four hours, CHF {{FULL_DAY}} the full day, Season 2026/27 — with video included. Lift passes and lunch are separate; you buy your own Flumserberg ticket.)*

## 5. Where do you meet, and is it easy to reach?

A meeting point you can't find eats into your paid time. *(Here: the COLORS restaurant door on Tannenbodenalp for the one- and two-hour lessons; for longer sessions we agree a point together. Flumserberg is in the Heidiland region of canton St. Gallen — by car from Zürich or St. Gallen, or by train to Unterterzen and the cable car up.)*

## 6. What are the cancellation and weather terms?

Mountains have bad days. Ask what happens if you cancel, or if the weather closes the lifts, before you pay — don't discover it afterwards. *(Ours are set out plainly in the [terms](/en/terms).)*

## A quick note on age

For children, also ask the minimum age and whether the format actually suits kids. *(Here: from age 8, with warm breaks and games built into children's lessons.)*
