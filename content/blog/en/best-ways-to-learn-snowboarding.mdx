"Do I book a lesson or figure it out myself?" It's the first question nearly everyone starting has. Here are the real options — and who each is for.

## The four options, no fluff

- **Private lesson.** An instructor just for you or your group (1 to 4). The pace and corrections are yours; you progress fast. The investment, not the cheapest.
- **Ski-school group lesson.** Cheaper per head and social, but you move at the group's pace and get little attention each.
- **A friend teaching you.** Free and fun, but a good rider can't always *explain* why the board does what it does — and often passes on their own quirks.
- **Videos and apps.** Good for ideas and theory at home; useless for correcting what you're doing wrong in the moment.

## Why private wins for most people

Learning to snowboard is mostly about losing the fear and finding a solid base turn as early as possible. That happens with someone who *sees* you, picks the one thing holding you back, and fixes it — ideally with video, so you see yourself. That's how I teach, in groups of 1 to 4 at the same level, filming along the way.

## When free is enough

If you already ride down comfortably and just want mileage and fun, a friend and YouTube will do. For the first days — or to get unstuck — it pays more to start well than to correct habits later.
