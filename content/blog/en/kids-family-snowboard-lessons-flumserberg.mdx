## Kids' lessons: how they run

They're private, in groups of 1 to 4 at the same level, and you book them. I match everything to the age: gentle terrain, sessions at their pace, and language they understand — no jargon. I film along the way and the video lands on your phone, which kids love. We don't go on a lift until the child is comfortable.

## Families: you can ride together

Because the price is per lesson (1 to 4 people), parents and children or siblings can ride the same session if they're at a similar level. If levels differ a lot, I'll tell you straight that you'll get more out of two slots — so nobody waits or gets frustrated.

## Age, safety and pace

Every child is different. Tell me the age and whether they've tried before, and I'll say honestly whether it's a good time or better to wait a little. The priority is always that they leave wanting to come back — not forcing it.
