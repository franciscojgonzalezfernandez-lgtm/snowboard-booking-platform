Here are the clear numbers, no small print. All are **per lesson** (not per person) and include 1 to 4 people from your group. 2026/27 season.

## Prices by duration (2026/27 season)

- **Confidence — 1 h — CHF 110**
- **First Day — 2 h — CHF 200**
- **Go Deep — 4 h — CHF 385**
- **All Day — 6 h — CHF 500**

The longer the session, the cheaper the hour: from CHF 110/h for a single hour to around CHF 83/h on a full day.

## Per lesson, not per person

You book the lesson for your group (1 to 4). If four of you come to the full-day session, that's CHF 125 each. It's why a private lesson in a group of friends gets close in price to a school group lesson — with all the attention on you.

## Larger groups and companies

The prices above are for the **private** lesson (1 to 4). For a bigger group — friends, a birthday or a company — I arrange it bespoke: no fixed cap on people and a **quote depending on the group**. Reach me via [contact](/en/contact) or give me a call and I'll put it together.

## What's included — and what isn't

Included: the lesson with a real coach, grouped by level, and the **video** that lands on your phone after. **Not** included: the lift pass — you buy your day pass yourself, directly in Flumserberg. No gear rental either.
