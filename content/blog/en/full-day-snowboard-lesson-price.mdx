The short answer: a full day is **CHF 500 for six hours on the snow** (Season 2026/27), and that price covers up to four riders. This post is the long answer — what that buys, how it compares, and who it's actually for.

## The full lesson price list

One flat price per lesson, for one to four riders (Season 2026/27):

- **One hour** — CHF 110 (CHF 110/hour)
- **Two hours** — CHF 200 (CHF 100/hour)
- **Four hours** — CHF 385 (about CHF 96/hour)
- **Full day, six hours** — CHF 500 (about CHF 83/hour)

The longer the lesson, the lower the hourly rate — the full day is the cheapest hour you can buy. And because the price is flat for up to four riders, a group of four splits CHF 500 into **CHF 125 each** for a whole day of coaching.

## What's included — and what isn't

**Included:** six hours of coaching, video filmed through the day and sent to your phone afterwards, and a meeting point we pick together (for the full day we're not tied to the COLORS door — we can start wherever the day's plan makes sense, and on a full day we can even ride other resorts in canton St. Gallen if we arrange it first).

**Not included:** your lift pass for Flumserberg, and lunch. You buy your own ski-area ticket; I'll tell you exactly which one you need when you book.

## Who the full day is for

- **Total beginners who want it to click for good.** Six hours is enough to get you off the flat, linking turns, and riding down comfortably — not "maybe", but for real, in one day.
- **Returning riders shaking off rust.** A full day rebuilds confidence and fixes the old habits in one go.
- **Intermediates chasing a specific goal** — carving cleanly, first park laps, riding switch. There's time to work it and lock it in the same day.
